# Get Hired Now

Income in weeks, not quarters. **Few constraints — the point is a good path to
income, not a good fit for the NYC search.** No NYC requirement, no $150K floor,
no archetype. Remote anywhere, another country, another currency, contract or
full-time, all fine. The only hard exclusions are work that trades hours for
minimum wage — delivery, retail, rideshare, warehouse — and anything with a
live-coding screen.

**This is not the PM search with a different passport.** Three different kinds of
thing live here, and most of them are not a product job:

- **Platforms you join once.** Not job postings at all. You register with the
  *network*, and work is routed to you afterwards — no interview loop. Fastest
  money on this page.
- **Things you can start alone.** Teaching, mentoring, advising. No employer, no
  hiring process, no permission needed.
- **Live roles.** Ordinary postings — and deliberately not only product ones.

Every URL below was checked on 2026-08-04 and resolves. `403` next to one means
the site blocks automated checks (Cloudflare), not that it is dead.

## Tier 1 — fastest to a first payment, and the best fit for your background

### Expert networks
You take paid 30-60 minute calls with investors and consultants who want
first-hand operator knowledge. **You set your own rate**; senior operators
commonly sit in the $200-500/hour band, billed per call. Onboarding is a profile
plus a compliance screen. This is the single fastest legitimate path here.

| Network | Sign up | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GLG | `https://glginsights.com/network/` (403 to bots) | Largest. No cap on the rate you set. |
| AlphaSights | `https://www.alphasights.com/experts/` | Fastest-moving, most service-led. |
| Guidepoint | `https://advisors.guidepoint.com/` | Broad, high call volume. |
| Third Bridge | `https://www.thirdbridge.com/experts/` | Strong on tech and industrials. |

**Why you specifically.** Four topics you can speak to that researchers actively
pay for, none of which most PMs have:
1. **Amazon Dash Button** — consumer IoT economics from the inside, 2015-16.
2. **Set-top box and semiconductor supply chain** — Conexant/NXP/Trident then Pace.
   Hardware analysts ask about this constantly and few operators are reachable.
3. **Selling security product to CISOs** — Reco.AI, Series A through $15M.
4. **Self-hosted AI infrastructure** — running a real multi-node GPU fleet, local
   inference economics vs. API pricing. This is a live investor question in 2026
   and you have hands-on numbers almost nobody else will give them.

Register with all four. They do not conflict, and call flow is lumpy.

### AI training / model evaluation marketplaces
Labs pay domain experts to write, grade and red-team model outputs. Onboarding is
an application plus a skills screen; work is asynchronous and genuinely remote.

with product management, AI infrastructure and enterprise security rather than
generic writing — the domain-expert rates are roughly double the generalist ones.

## Tier 1c — earning that is not a product job at all

The point of this tab is income, not a product title. These use the same fifteen
years without ever being "a PM role", and several are open to you tonight.

### Teach it
**Maven** — cohort-based courses, `https://maven.com/teach`. **No application and
no gate**: you build the course and run it. Instructors keep **90% of revenue**
minus Stripe fees, and Maven's own figure is that many earn **~$20,000 per
cohort**, several cohorts a year. Courses you could credibly run today:
- *Shipping AI products without an AI team* — the homelab is the curriculum.
- *Self-hosting your own inference stack* — almost nobody teaching this has run a
  real multi-node GPU fleet.
- *Product management for engineers*, in **Portuguese or Spanish** — a materially
  emptier market than the English one.

### Mentor it
Paid 1:1, low commitment, near-zero setup.
- **ADPList** — `https://adplist.org/become-a-mentor`
- **GrowthMentor** — `https://www.growthmentor.com/become-a-mentor/`

### Advise it
Paid advisory-board seats, typically cash plus a small equity grant.
- **Boardio** — `https://www.boardio.com/advisors`. Free to join *and* free to
  apply to open searches. Fintech, SaaS and **cross-border e-commerce** are the
  sectors that formalise paid advisor packages, and cross-border is exactly your
  US↔Brazil edge.
- **Connectd** — `https://www.connectd.com/us/advisors`, fractional execs and
  advisors across 80+ countries.

### Sell the thing only you can say
Market-entry and localisation advisory: a US company going into Brazil, or a
Brazilian company coming into the US. Product, GTM and regulatory judgement in
both languages. Sell it through Catalant and Business Talent Group below, and put
it on every expert-network profile — it is a niche with almost no competition,
not a generic consulting pitch.

## Deliberately excluded

Rideshare, delivery, warehouse, retail and call-centre work. Also excluded:
"training data" platforms paying under $25/hour for generalist tasks — the
domain-expert tier is worth four times as much for the same effort. And straight
translation or interpreting work, which pays a fraction of what a bilingual
*operator* call pays.
